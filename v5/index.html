How we run creative optimization

🎮 Video and playables are your best converting formats. Playables lead, top videos pair as end cards. Send a fresh playable batch every few weeks and we handle the rest.

Refresh cadence

  • New concept batch every 4–6 weeks per platform (2–3 concepts + size/hook variants)
  • Variant refreshes (hooks, end cards, CTA) in between, every 2 weeks
  • Always-on: at least one test cell live in every major campaign

Benchmarks we grade against

  • CVR vs campaign average, remove below 80% after a minimum test spend
  • D1/D7 ROAS per creative, high CVR that buys non-payers doesn't survive
  • Hard floor, creatives at 1–2% ROAS are removed to protect the blend

Test protocol

  • Each new asset is loaded paired with winning interactive end-cards
  • Top winning creatives are kept while non-winners are rotated out for new creatives
  • Localized creatives are added to geo-specific campaigns

Where we need Atlas

  • A fresh playable batch every few weeks. This is the highest-impact ask.
  • Two new iOS creative tests.
  • DE/IT localized variants (text + voiceover).
  • A rotation calendar set up with your new creative lead.

You beat your top rival on cost

Against Competitor 1, your closest auction rival, Atlas Earth buys cheaper on both platforms: $22 vs $28 on iOS and $12 vs $36 on Android, at higher IPM. You are winning the head-to-head.

The field looks cheap for a reason

Competitors 2 to 4 show very low CPI and high IPM, but that is cheaper, lower-intent inventory on different ROAS models. Not apples to apples, so read it as context, not a target.

Retention is the gap to close

Competitor 1 holds 12.1% D7 on both platforms while Atlas Earth sits at 9.4% iOS and 5.1% Android. This is exactly what the retention and Payer work is aimed at.
